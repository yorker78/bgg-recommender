Board Game Recommender

Pick a few games you like. A two-tower neural network, trained on 19 million BoardGameGeek ratings, turns your picks into one "ideal game" and finds the games closest to it. It runs entirely in your browser.

Game data, and the model built from it, come from the Board Games Database from BoardGameGeek by threnjen, shared here under CC BY-SA 3.0. Not affiliated with BoardGameGeek. It knows games published up to 2021.
